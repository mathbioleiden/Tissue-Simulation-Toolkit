Snapshots of simulations of single-cell migration on ECM with different stiffness levels. The arrow indicates the VEGF gradient direction. Time progresses from left to right. On soft ECM, the cell exhibits limited migration, while on stiff ECM, it moves directionally toward the VEGF source. In both soft and stiff ECM, remodeling occurs, as fiber densification at the rear of the cell and fiber reorientation around the cell. Fibers are drawn as gray lines and cross-links between fibers as green lines. The cell is drawn in red and focal adhesions are drawn as green spheres. The size of the sphere is purely visual and indicative of the number of bound integrins in that FA. Notably, a FA does not cover multiple lattice sites
